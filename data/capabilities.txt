Capabilities:
- Load `.txt` and `.md` files from the `data/` directory.
- Split documents into overlapping chunks for better retrieval.
- Embed chunks using a small sentence-transformer model.
- Store and load a FAISS vector index locally under `store/faiss_index`.
- Query with either OpenAI, Ollama, or a stub fallback LLM for offline smoke tests.
- Print the top retrieved sources alongside the answer.
